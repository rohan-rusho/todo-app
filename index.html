    Good to know

    FAQ.

    Where are my tasks saved?

    In your own browser (local storage). They survive closing the tab and coming back — but they live on this device only.

    Do I need an account?

    No. No sign-up, no sync, no email — just open the page and start listing.

    Can I edit a task?

    Yes — double-click a task (or press Enter on it) to edit it in place. Press Enter to save, Escape to cancel.

    Is there a limit?

    No hard limit — add as many tasks as you like.
